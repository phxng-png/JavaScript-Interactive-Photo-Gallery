The tenth world

Death is not the end.

Valheim is a survival and exploration game set in a procedurally generated world inspired by Norse mythology. Shipwrecked with nothing but a stone axe, you gather, craft, and build your way toward a place worth defending.

  • 01

    Survive

    Hunt, forage, and cook. Keep a fire burning through the rain, the cold, and whatever is watching from the treeline.

  • 02

    Build

    Raise a longhouse, a farm, and a palisade. Every beam, gate, and hearth is placed by your hand.

  • 03

    Explore

    Follow the coast, cross open water, and push into lands that were not meant for you.

Read the land

Four biomes. Four kinds of trouble.

The tenth world is generous until it is not. Learn what each land asks of you before you make camp.

Meadows

Start here

Sunlit grass, quiet water, and your first safe fire. A gentle start before the world shows its teeth.

Black Forest

Danger rising

Old pines, copper veins, and trolls large enough to redecorate a clearing. Watch the treeline.

Swamp

Danger severe

Cold water, drowned halls, and draugr that do not tire. The iron here is worth the rot.

Plains

Danger deadly

Golden grass hides fuling camps, tar pits, and the occasional rampaging lox. Keep your escape route.

Your story to write

Build. Sail. Rise.

There is no single path through Valheim — only the one you make.

Build

A hall to come home to

Raise a timber hall, a farm, and a palisade. Every beam is yours to place, from a first campfire to a fortress that can hold back a troll.

Sail

Chase the horizon

Load the hold and follow the shore — or trust the wind and cross open water. Longships carry ore, crew, and the promise of a new coast.

Fight

Stand before the Forsaken

Summon Eikthyr, The Elder, and the other Forsaken. Each is a test of your gear, your preparation, and your nerve.
